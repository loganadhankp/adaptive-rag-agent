# TechVision Inc. Employee Handbook

Welcome to TechVision Inc.! This handbook provides an overview of our company policies, benefits, and expectations.

## 1. Company Overview
TechVision Inc. is a leading technology consulting company founded in 2015. We specialize in digital transformation, cloud infrastructure, and AI solutions for enterprise clients worldwide. 

### Mission & Values
**Mission:** To empower organizations through innovative technology solutions that drive sustainable growth.
**Values:**
- Innovation: We embrace change and constantly seek better ways to solve problems.
- Integrity: We are transparent, honest, and ethical in all our dealings.
- Collaboration: We believe the best results come from working together.
- Excellence: We are committed to delivering the highest quality work.

## 2. Leave Policy
TechVision Inc. provides a generous leave policy to ensure our employees maintain a healthy work-life balance.

- **Annual Leave:** Employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
- **Sick Leave:** Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
- **Personal Leave:** Employees are entitled to 5 days of personal leave per year for emergencies or personal matters.
- **Eligibility:** Full-time employees are eligible for leave benefits immediately upon hire. Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rata basis.
- **Carryover Rules:** Employees may carry over up to 5 days of unused annual leave to the following calendar year. Unused sick and personal leave cannot be carried over.
- **Approval Process:** All annual leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance and approved by the direct manager.

## 3. Remote Work Policy
We support a flexible working environment.

- **Hybrid Model:** Employees are expected to work from the office 3 days a week and may work remotely for 2 days a week.
- **Equipment Allowance:** TechVision provides a one-time $500 allowance for setting up a home office.
- **VPN Requirements:** All remote work must be conducted over the company VPN to ensure data security.
- **Core Hours:** Core working hours are from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone, during which employees must be available for meetings and collaboration.

## 4. Code of Conduct
- **Professional Behavior:** Treat all colleagues, clients, and partners with respect. Bullying or harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.
- **Dress Code:** Our office dress code is business casual. For client meetings, formal business attire may be required.
- **Confidentiality:** Employees must protect company and client confidential information at all times.
- **Conflict of Interest:** Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to HR.

## 5. Benefits
- **Health Insurance:** Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance with premiums 80% covered by the company.
- **401k Matching:** TechVision matches 401k contributions up to 6% of your base salary.
- **Professional Development:** Employees receive a $2,000 annual budget for courses, certifications, or conferences.
- **Gym Membership:** Reimbursement of up to $50/month for gym or fitness memberships.

## 6. Onboarding Process
- **First Week:** Focuses on company orientation, IT setup, and introductory meetings with team members.
- **Buddy System:** New hires are assigned a "buddy" from another team to help them navigate company culture.
- **90-Day Review:** A formal review with the manager at the end of the probationary period to assess integration and performance.

## 7. Performance Reviews
- **Bi-Annual Reviews:** Formal performance reviews occur in June and December.
- **Self-Assessment:** Employees complete a self-assessment detailing their achievements and challenges.
- **Manager Assessment:** Managers evaluate performance based on goals and core values.
- **Goal Setting:** Goals for the next half-year are collaboratively set using the SMART framework.

## 8. IT Policies
- **Password Requirements:** Passwords must be at least 14 characters, changed every 90 days, and require MFA.
- **Acceptable Use:** Company devices are for business use. Minimal personal use is permitted if it does not interfere with work.
- **Data Security:** Phishing simulations are conducted monthly. Never share passwords or leave laptops unlocked in public spaces.
- **BYOD Policy:** Personal devices used for work must have company mobile device management (MDM) software installed.
